About the Author

Andres "Andy" Roosna
Founder & Principal, OLS Group

Operational Leadership Systems Practitioner
36 Countries • Corporate Operations • Finance • M&A • Organizational Transformation

Andres "Andy" Roosna is the Founder and Principal of Operational Leadership Systems (OLS Group), a consulting practice focused on helping organizations improve visibility, trust, ownership, accountability, execution, and adaptability.

Over a career spanning leadership and operational roles across 36 countries, Andy has worked in finance, corporate operations, government contracting, mergers and acquisitions, organizational transformation, construction operations, and complex leadership environments.

His work focuses on helping leaders understand what has changed inside the operating system of their organization before deciding what should happen next.

The OLS Canon is a collection of operational leadership doctrine developed from decades of practical experience building teams, solving execution problems, leading change initiatives, and supporting leaders responsible for complex organizations.
