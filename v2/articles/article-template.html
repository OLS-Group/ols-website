About the Author

Andres “Andy” Roosna Founder & Principal, OLS Group

Andres “Andy” Roosna is the Founder & Principal of OLS Group, where he develops operational leadership systems for organizations facing complexity, growth, execution drag, and leadership visibility challenges. His background spans global operations, finance, corporate restructuring, government contracting, M&A advisory, construction operations, and high-risk leadership environments across 36 countries.

To explore more OLS Group perspectives on leadership, trust, visibility, accountability, and execution systems, follow Andres “Andy” Roosna and OLS Group on LinkedIn or visit the OLS Group website.
